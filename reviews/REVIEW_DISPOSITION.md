# Reviewer Disposition

This directory retains the three full initial reports that were captured as files. The Domain Expert and Competition Judge review findings were captured in the build work log and are summarized in `REGRESSION_REPORT.md`; they are not reconstructed as invented reviewer transcripts.

Initial reviewer FAIL is expected: each report records the snapshot that exposed a bypass. Closure is established by the production validators and the two executable adversarial suites, rather than by editing historical reports to say PASS.
